## 3. INTERPRETIVE CONVENTIONS (how Zach actually reads)
1. **Span math, two modes.** "X days later / after" = end-exclusive. "A span of X days" = inclusive of both endpoints (always exactly 1 more than "later"). Zach picks whichever lands on a loaded number — check BOTH and label which mode hit. Same for weeks ("span of 187 weeks") and months (222 months from 9/11 to the pandemic).
2. **Age-day framing.** Events land on "the Nth day of [person]'s age" — days since last birthday (birthday = day 1). Also check days UNTIL next birthday. Birthdays and death anniversaries of principals (owners, founders, popes, the Superior General b. Nov 12 1948) are anchor dates.
3. **Prime & composite indices.** Any prime → its index is a second number to read (67 = 19th prime → read 19 too). Also triangular numbers (used 100+ times in the books): N = kth triangular → read k (253 = 22nd triangular → 22 = JESUITS-cluster). 666 = 36th triangular is his flagship example.
4. **Mirror pairs.** 47↔74, 38↔83, 34↔43, 56↔65, 112↔211, 13↔31 — reflections count as the same family.
5. **Name-of-the-messenger.** The reporter, sheriff, official, or bid-breaker's name encodes the story (matches Tony's McCarthy lesson and no-hitter rule). Always cipher the peripheral names, not just the principal.
6. **Number-spelled-out layer.** The WORD form of a number carries values ("FORTY-TWO" = 74 Rev, "EVENT TWO ZERO ONE" = 222 Ord). When a number is the story, cipher its spelling.
7. **Tribute logic.** Outcomes "honor" an entity when the final counter, span, or score lands on that entity's value on a related date (founding anniversaries, city/venue geography — respects state-routing gates). One clean tribute beats five weak matches.
8. **Cluster reading.** Numbers arrive in themes: death cluster (38/44/34/43/59/67/83/97/118), Jesuit cluster (84/78/103/144/54/153/72/201/191/56/187/65/79), masonic cluster (33/42/96/147/48/59/58/139/113/175), civic cluster (49/77/40/84/147). Two numbers from the SAME cluster in one ladder = the story; scattered singles = noise.
9. **Verification humility.** Zach's book claims were engine-checked at mining time; anything quoted from his daily posts still gets recomputed before use (standing thread-builder rule).

2. **COUNTER layer:** entering stat counters (season AND career) checked for landings — entering value, +1 through +4, and projected realistic finals — against day field + core table. Clean entering source: Baseball-Reference (never includes current day).
3. **AGE layer (mandatory, per player):** age in years · days-of-life (+span) · days since last birthday · age-day (since+1) · days to next birthday (+span).
